# Supporting independent evidence for MANOSUBE

MANOSUBE preserves objectives, authorization boundaries, completion evidence and unresolved differences across replaceable development agents. The public implementation and releases make the design inspectable. Its general reliability benefit and cost-effectiveness remain questions for measurement.

Support is sought for three concrete outputs: independent reproduction reports including failures; a preregistered real-agent false-completion comparison with raw evidence and measured cost; and simpler installation/documentation with reproducible Linux quality checks. The [quickstart](../examples/01_minimal_kernel_cycle/README.md) and [evaluation protocol](../examples/false_completion/PROTOCOL.md) describe the starting point.

Publish progress against these outputs, including unresolved defects, the version tested, checks that actually ran, real spending records when available, and the help independent operators needed. Unmeasured spending or impact is unknown; no return-on-investment figure is promised. The evaluation budget and dates are to be set from actual operator/account constraints before paid runs.

You can contribute a reproduction without donating: [Issue #100](https://github.com/manosube/manosube-agent-civilization-os/issues/100). For financial support, use the repository's existing Sponsor entry. Sponsorship does not establish technical validity, purchase evidence acceptance or change the project's Human authority policy.

Current positioning: an open implementation and evaluation project for evidence-backed development governance. Production-wide safety, causal superiority and standards compliance are not established by a release tag or sponsorship.
